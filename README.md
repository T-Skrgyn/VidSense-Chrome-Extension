## ✨ Features

* 🛍️ **On Market Places** → shows product reviews & unboxings
* 📚 **On Education Website** → shows tutorials & explainers  
* 📰 **On news sites & blogs** → shows related video coverage
* 🌍 **Works on any website** → detects context automatically
* 🌐 **15+ languages** — Hindi, English, Tamil, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, and more
* 📊 **Flexible sorting** — Most Viewed, Latest, Most Relevant, Top Rated
* ⏱️ **Duration filtering** — Short (<4min), Medium (4–20min), Long (>20min)
* 🔐 **Zero sign-up** — no authentication required, just install and go
* ⚡ **Lightweight** — only 17 KiB, Manifest V3 compliant
* 🚀 **Fast** — instant recommendations with minimal browser impact

## 📥 Installation

### Install from Chrome Web Store (Recommended)

1. **[Click here to visit Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/vidsense-%E2%80%93-context-aware/calfofibblgehdidaledjgplanlaapda)**
2. Click **"Add to Chrome"**
3. Confirm installation
4. Click the VidSense icon in your toolbar to start!

---

## 🎯 How to Use

1. **Visit any website** (Amazon, Wikipedia, news site, blog, etc.)
2. **Click the VidSense icon** in your toolbar
3. **VidSense analyzes the page** and detects what you're viewing
4. **See relevant YouTube videos instantly** with filters for language, sorting, and duration
5. **Click any video to watch** directly on YouTube

**Examples:**
- 🛍️ Browsing iPhone 15 → get unboxing & review videos
- 📚 Reading Python tutorial → get video explanations & walkthroughs
- 📰 Reading news article → get video coverage of the same topic
